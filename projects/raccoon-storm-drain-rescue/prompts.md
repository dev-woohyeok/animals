# Only Her Face Above Water

> A raccoon's desperate fight for survival in a flooded storm drain during a heavy rainstorm. A man discovers her just in time and races against rising water to save her life.

**Total Duration:** 90 seconds (8 scenes)

## Production Notes

### Visual Consistency
- All scenes shot in 1st person POV (hands/arms only visible)
- Raccoon: gray-brown fur, black mask, striped tail
- Location: Seattle suburbs, USA
- Weather: Heavy rainstorm (scenes 1-7), sunny morning (scene 8)

### Audio Guidelines
- No background music throughout
- Natural sounds: rain, water, breathing, raccoon whimpering
- Scene 8: Bird sounds, wind, leaves rustling

### Emotional Arc
```
Scene 1-2: Shock/Discovery (90%)
Scene 3-4: Anxiety/Fear (95-98%)
Scene 5-6: Urgency/Hope (85%)
Scene 7-8: Relief/Joy (90%)
```
