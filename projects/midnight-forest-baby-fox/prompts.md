# The Baby Fox I Met in the Midnight Forest

> A baby fox comes crying to a camper's tent at midnight, leading him to her dying mother. After rescuing the kit from wolves, the man decides to take her home as family.

> 한밤중 캠핑 텐트에 울며 찾아온 아기 여우가 남자를 엄마에게 이끌지만, 이미 엄마는 늑대에게 당한 뒤였다. 늑대 무리로부터 아기 여우를 구해 함께 집으로 떠나는 이야기.

- All scenes: First person POV handheld phone footage (amateur style)

- Camera holder never visible, only hands
- No background music, natural ambient sound only
- Simple direct sentences, no cinematic/dramatic expressions
- Heavy breathing, footsteps, natural sounds audible
